# Ad Mastery — Living Playbook

> Last consolidated: seed · Sources analyzed: 0
> Policy: the LLM **reads this file before every ideation call** and **rewrites/appends to it after every analysis** (Learn tab) and after every approved production. It is a living document, not a blueprint — patterns below are hypotheses that get reinforced, refined, or retired as evidence accumulates.

---

## 1. Hook Patterns (0–3s)
- **Pattern-interrupt:** open mid-action or with a frame that makes no sense yet; resolve within 0.8–2s.
- **Bold claim + instant proof tease:** state the outcome, flash receipts within 2s.
- **POV immersion:** "POV:" framing drops the viewer inside the story immediately.
- **Negative hook:** name the pain before the product exists ("You're still doing X?").
- **Question loop:** open a question the video does not close until late.
- Rule: the first frame must be readable at 10% brightness on a phone screen.

## 2. Structure & Beats
- **15s:** Hook → Problem → Product-as-hero → Proof → single CTA.
- **30s:** Hook → Agitate → Reveal → 3 benefit beats → Social proof → CTA.
- **60s:** Story arc with a relatable protagonist; product enters ~40% in; payoff montage; CTA stinger.
- One idea per scene. If a scene carries two ideas, split it.
- The product must be recognizable by scene 2 at the latest.

## 3. Pacing & Editing
- Cut every **1.2–2.0s** for UGC; **2.5–4s** for cinematic brand work.
- Every cut changes shot size *or* angle — never cut to the same framing.
- Cut on VO emphasis beats, not only on music.
- Open a loop at ~8s (question or visual promise), close it by ~20s.
- End-card holds exactly long enough to read the CTA once (≈1.2s per beat of copy).

## 4. Voiceover & Copy
- 2nd person, present tense. "You" arrives before "we".
- Read-aloud test: no sentence longer than 14 words in the first 15s.
- VO carries emotion; on-screen text carries facts. Never fully duplicate both.
- The pivot phrase to the CTA starts *before* the CTA section ("…so here's what I do now").

## 5. CTA Engineering
- CTA = **1 action + 1 reason + 1 urgency cue.** Anything more leaks clicks.
- Reserve the last ~15% of runtime as CTA territory.
- Visual CTA: product in hand + end card that repeats the *exact* spoken words.
- Split test: soft CTA ("link's below") vs hard CTA ("get 20% off today") — log which wins.

## 6. Visual Grammar
- Faces beat objects in the first second (UGC); spectacle beats faces for brand films.
- Cast and locations are **minted once, reused by ID** — on Higgsfield MCP always referenced via `@elements`.
- Keep a consistent light logic per world: one key direction per location.
- Abnormalities to QC every generated frame: hands/fingers, text legibility, warped logos, extra objects, character drift between scenes.

## 7. Compliance & Taste
- No unverifiable superlatives. Show, don't claim.
- Proof beats adjectives: receipts, timers, before/after, counters.
